The product in action

From measured lift to next-set cue.

Peso keeps coaching connected to the evidence: the athlete can inspect the movement, understand the observation, and leave with one practical focus instead of a wall of scores.

Coaching system

Explainable cues, built from visible evidence.

Peso treats tracking as the evidence layer. Coaching is the product outcome: a measured observation, a confidence-aware explanation, and one useful action for the next set.

Implemented

01 · OBSERVE

Follow the movement

Pose and visible-collar tracking preserve the movement evidence through each rep.

Implemented

02 · MEASURE

Find the pattern

Rep analysis evaluates depth, torso position, consistency, and recording quality.

Implemented

03 · COACH

Choose a focused cue

Transparent rules produce practical feedback or recording guidance when confidence is limited.

04 · CONTEXT

Learn from recent lifts

Matching saved lifts can add detected rep-count context. Broader technique personalization remains planned.

Build decision

Rules plus history before machine learning
