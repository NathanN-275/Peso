# Peso coaching positioning

## Audience and promise

Peso is for new lifters learning technique and self-coached lifters who want a clearer focus for their next set. The lead promise is **Improve each set**: Peso connects visible squat analysis to focused coaching cues without special equipment.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** Leave each analysis with one useful focus for the next set.
2. **Method:** Peso turns measured observations into explainable coaching cues.
3. **Proof:** Rep results remain connected to pose and visible-bar tracking in the video.
4. **Continuity:** Saved Lifts preserve analysis, cues, and workout facts for later review.

Tracking is evidence for the coaching result, not the primary marketing outcome.

## Voice

- Supportive and plain enough for a new lifter.
- Specific enough for a performance-minded, self-coached lifter.
- Confident about implemented measurements and direct about uncertainty.
- Focused on one practical next action instead of a wall of scores.

## Approved coaching claims

Peso can describe:

- whether analyzed reps reached, missed, or had uncertain depth;
- depth and torso-position consistency across a set;
- forward-lean and inconsistent-torso observations when detected;
- recording-quality limits and concrete guidance for a better recording;
- limited detected rep-count context from recent saved videos with the same exercise and camera view.

Example flow:

- **Observation:** Inconsistent depth.
- **Evidence:** Some reps finished above the deepest rep.
- **Next-set cue:** Try to match your deepest reps.

## Claim limits

Do not claim that Peso:

- guarantees strength, technique improvement, or training results;
- prevents injury, diagnoses conditions, or provides medical advice;
- replaces a qualified coach;
- provides strength programming or broad personalized technique trends today;
- can judge a lift confidently when recording or tracking evidence is limited.

“Get stronger” may describe the athlete’s motivation, never a guaranteed Peso result.

## Surface roles

- **Netlify marketing site:** Lead with the next-set coaching outcome, show the observation-to-cue flow, and use tracking as visible proof.
- **GitHub Pages project log:** Explain how coaching is built, distinguish implemented and planned capabilities, link decisions and code, and preserve architecture and progress reporting.
