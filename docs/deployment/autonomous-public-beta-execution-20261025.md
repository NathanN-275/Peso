# Autonomous Public Web Beta execution

Accepted by Nathan on October 25, 2026 in this chat. Nathan requested continued
autonomous work toward public deployment, recommended decisions and as many
subagents as useful. Nathan then explicitly approved running the planned 40
regression cases, relevant existing checks, and local builds/audits, fixing and
rerunning failures as needed, with public exposure, destructive data changes
and new costs reserved for final approval.

## Settled release decisions

- Deliver the combined Marketing Site and functional authenticated Web App at
  `usepeso.com`, with US-IP-restricted open signup/upload and supported side-view
  squat analysis, as accepted in ADR 0018. Information-only marketing remains
  preparation for this release.
- Preserve production/staging boundaries, existing projects, accounts and Saved
  Lifts. Use the reviewed existing production database/API/worker binding;
  staging data is not promoted.
- Follow recommended technical choices autonomously and prepare a complete
  private release. Parallel source preparation can continue while a gate is
  blocked; dependent live acceptance cannot be claimed passed.
- Keep CRITICAL and untreated HIGH findings blocking, the web no-HIGH-exceptions
  guard, existing CI and evidence requirements. A raw HIGH finding is not
  cleared by a passing synthetic test or a lock-only change. A temporary HIGH
  treatment still requires exact finding/evidence/artifact/expiry approval and
  support from enforced policy under ADR 0019.
- Complete mandatory recovery, migration, geography, guardrail, two-owner,
  browser/accessibility and artifact acceptance in the launch plan. Collect
  matching evidence and retain failures. Reuse prior evidence only where its
  identities and coverage still apply.

## Execution and authority

Routine source/test authoring, local checks, local builds/audits, read-only
research and delegation now proceed without another routine decision question.
Use bounded commands, owned fixtures, sanitized logs and cleanup; record actual
commands, identities, failures and fixes. Execution approval is not permission
to weaken a failing check or silently amend security treatment.

Obtain input only for genuinely unavailable human/provider actions, exact new
spending, destructive data changes, or the action-time public transition after
the release/cost/rollback facts are ready. The prior two-hour paid worker trial
and monthly estimate are not perpetual paid resumption approval. Resolve the
observed worker-plan mismatch before any Blueprint synchronization; do not
silently downgrade a validated workload or activate a paid service.

## Decision tree and launch sequence

The release target and scope are settled. Next work is candidate-matched
security/runtime acceptance, credential disposition and revocable playback
preparation; then hosted authentication/recovery, fresh backup and reviewed
migrations, US admission and operational guardrails, production binding and
private build, two-owner acceptance, protected release and public transition.
Every live step retains its prerequisite gate. Public launch remains unaccepted
until all mandatory evidence and the final action-time confirmation exist.

The existing domain definitions in `CONTEXT.md` govern Marketing Site, Public
Web Beta, Web App, Production Backend, Student Environment and Saved Lift. No
new product vocabulary or scope was introduced by this autonomy decision.

The active all-14-gate tracker remains the operational status record. Historical
evidence packets are immutable; newer approvals supersede their future-work
wording without altering past grants or results.
