## Counterexample observed before grant repair

The initial full application-schema read-back differed from the source **only in grants to `anon` and `authenticated`**. Definitions, owners, RLS, policies, constraints, indexes and default privileges matched outside those grants. The difference covered 34 role/object pairs: twelve functions and five tables, each granted to both client roles more broadly than the snapshot permits.

This is consistent with automatic default grants on new Supabase objects: a restore can add the source's narrower grants without removing grants already created by the target's defaults. That mechanism is an inference from the exact diff and the observed target defaults, not a captured pre-creation default-ACL trace. Supabase documents these automatic grants and the separate effects of grants and RLS in [Securing your API](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/api/securing-your-api#default-privileges-for-new-tables-and-functions).
