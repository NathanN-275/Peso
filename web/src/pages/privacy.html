Beta draft for professional review.

This page is product copy for the private prototype and is not approved legal advice or launch-ready policy.

Peso is being prepared as a limited, free web beta with open signup through US IP addresses. IP location does not establish residency. This draft explains the intended data handling so testers can evaluate the experience before launch.

Information the beta expects to use

Account details such as email address and verification status; profile and optional workout details; squat videos and analysis results; security and reliability logs; and product analytics only after explicit consent.

How information is intended to be used

To check whether the signup IP is in the US, authenticate accounts, receive and analyze squat videos, display and save results, enforce beta capacity, protect the service, diagnose failures, and improve the experience with opt-in feedback.

Video and analysis data

Video uploads are intended to remain private to the account owner. The planned beta policy removes unsaved videos, analysis results, and history entries after 3 days from upload registration. Saved videos and results remain until you delete them. Videos will not be copied or retained for model training. Verification of deletion, the processor list, and legal review remain launch requirements.

Optional analytics

Peso intends to collect only a short list of product events after consent. Video names, URLs, frames, coaching results, and other video-derived data will not be sent to analytics. Session replay will remain disabled.

Your choices

You can decline optional analytics, discard an unsaved result, and request account or Saved Lift deletion through the support process that will be published before launch.

Contact

Email support@usepeso.com for support, privacy or terms questions, or account and Saved Lift deletion requests.
